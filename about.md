# About

**New Every Morning**

## Why this app exists

I love reading through my Bible. What I did not look forward to was starting the same plan over again, year after year.

So this plan runs four readings side by side, each at its own pace:

- **Old Testament:** 1–10 chapters a day, from the 748 chapters outside Psalms and Proverbs
- **Psalms:** one a day, from the 150 Psalms
- **Proverbs:** one a day, from the 31 chapters of Proverbs
- **New Testament:** 1–10 chapters a day, from the 260 chapters of the New Testament

Each reading moves forward day by day and begins again when it reaches the end. Because the four cycles are different lengths, they never line up the same way twice. The same day's combination won't repeat in your lifetime, and you may begin to see connections you missed before.

The rhythm reflects what I need. Proverbs comes around month after month, because I need wisdom and keep crying out to God for it. A psalm a day is my prayer book, a major pathway to connecting with my Father in heaven and a daily reminder of his character, his unchangeable nature and his ever-present help. The Old Testament carries me steadily from Genesis to Malachi, where I watch the same faithful God keep his promises to Abraham and his offspring across the generations, and every page leans forward toward the Messiah to come. The New Testament runs on from Matthew to Revelation and then begins again, keeping my eyes and my heart on the cross and on our risen Saviour.

## How it works

You choose a start date, the starting Psalm and Proverb, the starting book and chapter for the Old and New Testaments, and how many chapters of each to read per day. The app lays out the whole plan with dates and shows the average reading time for each day's selection. You decide whether to read all four passages at once or split them between morning and evening. Tick each day off as you finish it.

At any time, **Extend Plan** continues all four readings from where they stopped. If you fall behind, because life got busy or you spent a few weeks studying the book of John, **Catch Up** moves the days you missed, and everything after them, so the plan resumes today. You can't fall behind for good, so there's never a reason to be discouraged.

## It works with the Bible you already use

This app does not contain the Bible text. It holds the plan and your place in it, so you can read from whatever Bible you actually use: the one on your desk or bedside table, or a Bible app on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac.

Tap a reading and it opens in the Bible app you have chosen. Or leave your phone alone, turn your paper Bible to the four passages listed for the day, and come back only to tick the day off.

There is no feed, no advertising, no account to create, and nothing asking for your attention. Just today's four readings and a place to mark them done. Prefer no app at all? Four bookmarks in a paper Bible do much the same job.

Your plans and progress sync privately between your iPhone, iPad and Mac through your own iCloud account. Nothing is sent to me.
