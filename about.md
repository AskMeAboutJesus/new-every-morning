# About

**New Every Morning**

## Why this app exists

I love reading through my Bible. What I did not look forward to was starting the same plan over again, year after year.

So this plan runs four readings side by side, and they never line up the same way twice. The four readings are from the 748 chapters in the Old Testament (not including Psalms and Proverbs), the 150 Psalms, the 31 Proverbs, and the 260 New Testament chapters. The app generates a plan that includes (1-10) OT chapters, (1-10 NT chapters, a Psalm, and a Proverb. Each of these move forward day by day through the year. You pick the start book and chapter for the OT and NT and how many chapters to read. Because every day's reading is fresh, not repeating in your lifetime, you'll begin to see connections you may have missed before. Proverbs repeats month after month, because I need wisdom and keep crying out to God for it. A psalm a day is my prayer book — a major pathway to connecting with my Father in heaven, and a daily reminder of his character, his unchangeable nature and his ever-present help. The New Testament runs on from Matthew to Revelation and then begins again, keeping my eyes and my heart on the cross and on our risen Saviour.

## It works with the Bible you already use

This app does not contain the Bible text. It holds the plan and your place in it, so you can read from whatever Bible you actually use — the one on your desk or your bedside table. Or from a Bible app on your phone, iPad, or Mac.

Tap a reading and it opens in the Bible app you have chosen. Or leave your phone alone, turn your paper Bible to the four passages listed for the day, and come back only to tick the day off.

There is no feed, no advertising, no account to create and nothing asking for your attention. Just today's four readings, and a place to mark them done.

## How it works

You choose a start date and how many chapters of the Old and New Testament to read each day. The app lays out the whole plan with dates, and includes the average reading time for each day's selection. You decide if you want to read all four sections at once or split into your morning and evening. Tick each day off as you study it.

At any time, **Extend Plan** carries all four readings on from where they stopped. If you fall behind, **Catch Up** moves the day you missed, and everything after it, to a new date.

Your plans and progress sync privately between your iPhone, iPad and Mac through your own iCloud account. Nothing is sent to me.
