# About

**New Every Morning**

## Why this app exists

I love reading through my Bible. What I do not look forward to is starting the same plan over again, year after year.
This plan runs four parallel strands, each at its own pace:

So this plan runs four parallel strands, each at its own pace:

- **Old Testament:** 1–10 chapters a day, from the 748 chapters, not including Psalms and Proverbs
- **Psalms:** One a day, from the 150 Psalms
- **Proverbs:** One a day, from the 31 chapters of Proverbs
- **New Testament:** 1–10 chapters a day, from the 260 chapters of the New Testament

Since the four strands are different lengths, after they cycle through, they restart and never line up the same way twice. The same day's combination will never repeat in your lifetime. This keeps your reading fresh and may enable you to see new connections you might have missed repeating the same plan each year.

Each reading moves forward day by day and begins again when it reaches the end. Because the four cycles are different lengths, they never line up the same way twice. The same day's combination won't repeat in your lifetime, and you may begin to see connections you missed before.

The combination of the four strands reflects what I need. Proverbs repeats each month, because I need wisdom and desire it more than gold. One psalm each day is a major pathway to connecting with my Father in heaven and a daily reminder of his character, his unchangeable nature, and his ever-present help. The Psalms are also my worship and prayer book. The Old Testament carries me steadily from Genesis to Malachi, where I watch the same faithful God keep his promises to Abraham and his offspring across the generations. Every page leans forward toward the coming Messiah. The New Testament runs from Matthew to Revelation, keeping my eyes and my heart on the cross and on our risen Saviour, helping me to walk in a manner worthy of him and strengthens me according to his glorious might.

## How it works

- Choose a start date
- Add the starting Psalm and Proverb, the starting book and chapter for the Old and New Testaments
- Select how many chapters of each (OT & NT) to read per day
- The app lays out the plan, for the year, with dates, and shows the average reading time for each day's selection. You decide whether to read all four passages at once or split them between morning and evening
- Tick each day off as you finish it
- At any time, **Extend Plan** adds an extra year of readings to your plan, extending all four readings from where the last years plan ended
- If you fall behind, **Catch Up** shifts the dates you missed, and everything after them, so the plan resumes the day you want it to. You can't fall behind, so there's never a reason to be discouraged

## It works with the Bible you already use

This app does not contain any Bible text. It holds the plan and your place in it, from whatever Bible you actually use: the one on your desk or bedside table, or a Bible app on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac.

Tap a reading and it opens in the Bible app you have chosen. Or leave your phone alone and read the day’s four strands from your paper Bible, and come back to New Every Morning to check off the day as completed.

There is no feed, no advertising, no account to create, and no distractions. Just today's four readings and a place to mark them completed.

Your plans and progress sync privately between your iPhone, iPad, and Mac, through your own iCloud account. Nothing is shared with me.
